# TypeSign personality quiz for iPhone and sharing result cards

TypeSign offers a 15-question, everyday-scenario quiz for exploring a 16-type personality profile. It also includes Big Five and career-interest quizzes, saved summaries and daily reflection cards. This first-party guide is maintained by the app operator and describes the US App Store listing for version 1.0.3, checked on 15 November 2026.

[Download TypeSign on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6797152806)

## Start with a question you want to reflect on

You do not need a label that explains your whole life. A useful starting point is a concrete question: which descriptions feel familiar, which do not, and what example from your own week supports that judgment? Quiz output is a prompt for reflection, not a diagnosis or a measurement of ability.

The basic quiz summaries are free. You can view your result, keep a history, explore the type library and use the app's shareable personality cards. Big Five and career-interest summaries offer different views of preferences; deep reports use the existing Pro membership.

## Share a result without turning it into a verdict

Use the result-card sharing feature, choose your destination in the system share interface, and confirm delivery there. Opening or cancelling that interface does not prove a message was sent. Before sending, decide which result and image you actually want another person to see.

Two-person match cards use types selected in the app. A card is not evidence that the other person's report was fetched or synced. Scores and descriptions are entertainment and self-reflection references, not scientific compatibility predictions.

One conversation prompt you can adapt:

> “This description sounds familiar to me because ___. Which part fits you, and which part doesn't?”

This is a suggested prompt, not a customer testimonial or an observed outcome.

## What is free and what uses Pro?

The public listing describes basic quiz summaries as free. Existing monthly or yearly auto-renewable TypeSign Pro membership includes deep reports, a weekly recap, tomorrow's card preview, past readings and additional card styles. Prices and trial eligibility are shown on the App Store purchase sheet. This guide does not promise a price, trial or availability in every territory.

## Common questions

**Is TypeSign a mental-health diagnosis or career qualification?** No. Quizzes, matching and zodiac-inspired content are for entertainment and self-reflection, not professional advice.

**Can I see earlier results?** The public version includes saved quiz history. Pro daily-reading history is a separate feature; do not confuse free quiz summaries with all Pro features being free.

**Is this an Android download?** This guide links to the iPhone App Store listing. It does not provide an Android package.

**Which devices and languages?** The checked listing requires iOS 17 or later and lists English and Simplified Chinese. Check your local store for current compatibility and availability.
