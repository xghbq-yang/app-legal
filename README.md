The descriptions refer to publicly listed TypeSign 1.0.3 in the US and SuperStar 1.2 in mainland China, checked on 15 November 2026. Both listings require iOS 17 or later. Store availability can vary by territory. Test builds and unreleased changes are not advertised here.

TypeSign quizzes, match cards and zodiac-inspired content are for entertainment and self-reflection, not psychological diagnosis, career qualification or professional advice. SuperStar does not provide cloud sync or an AI coach.
