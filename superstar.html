适用范围与联系

本政策适用于超级星（SuperStar）。开发与运营主体：杨星星 / Yang Xingxing。生效日期：2026-11-05。隐私联系邮箱：xghbqyxx@163.com。

超级星是一款阶段计划与成长记录工具。核心功能无需账号，可在本机离线使用；你主动打开课程网站、选择云盘文件或分享内容时，相关系统和外部服务可能使用网络。

保存在本机的信息

你创建或导入的目标、任务、时间安排、计时与补记、完成状态、笔记、周复盘，以及你选择的图片附件，保存在应用的本地数据中。主题和界面语言等偏好也保存在本机。

图片由应用在本机处理并压缩保存，保存的是压缩副本，不是原图备份。我们不要求你提供姓名、手机号或注册账号来使用这些核心功能。

开发者不自动接收你的记录

当前版本没有开发者运营的记录服务器，不自动上传你的计划、笔记、图片或使用记录；没有接入第三方广告、分析、追踪或AI处理SDK。

本地提醒在你允许后由设备系统安排，不通过我们的服务器发送。你可以在系统设置中管理通知权限；关闭提醒不影响本地记录。

文件导入、备份、恢复与系统备份

只有在你选择文件后，应用才读取相应计划、备份或图片。文件提供方可能是本机、系统云盘或你配置的第三方文件服务，其文件访问和网络传输受系统及该服务的规则约束。

导出的JSON备份包含计划、记录、笔记、周复盘和压缩图片。该备份没有应用单独设置的密码加密；图片以可还原的数据形式包含其中。请按敏感文件保管，不要公开上传。

恢复或课程内容更新前，应用可能保留本机恢复副本以保护已有记录。系统备份、设备迁移或你选择的云盘可能保存副本，具体由平台版本、设备设置和相关服务决定。Android当前配置关闭系统自动备份，手动导出与设备迁移的实际行为仍以系统为准。我们不承诺所有数据永远不可能进入任何云端。

主动分享与外部学习链接

当你使用系统分享或导出文件时，由你选择接收方和保存位置。接收应用、云盘或平台会按照其自身规则处理你主动提供的内容；应用不会替你自动发布到社交平台。

1.1版本中的网页链接由系统浏览器打开。从1.2起，学习材料、任务说明、产出说明和笔记预览中的普通网页链接会在你主动点击后优先在应用内浏览器打开，也可选择系统浏览器；网页需要网络。应用内网页使用临时会话，Cookie和缓存不写入计划备份，应用不持久保存网页浏览历史。外部网站仍可能处理网络请求、账号或浏览信息，适用其自身隐私政策；超级星不会把你的本地笔记或图片自动附加到网页请求。访问本政策或支持网页也受相应托管服务的规则约束。

保留、修改和删除

你可以修改本地笔记、记录及移除附件，并管理通知权限。删除当前附件或修改当前数据，不会自动清除此前导出的文件、分享给他人的内容、系统或云盘备份，以及恢复前的本地副本。

需要清除全部本机资料时，请使用操作系统提供的删除应用及其数据方式，并分别处理你保留的导出文件和备份；仅移除应用而保留数据的系统选项不等同于清除数据。我们没有你的本地记录副本，无法从服务器远程找回或删除它们。

支持邮件、变更与你的选择

如果你主动发送支持邮件，我们会处理你在邮件中提供的地址、问题描述和附件，用于回复与排查。请不要发送身份证、密码、完整私密备份或不必要的个人信息；你可通过同一邮箱请求处理你提交的客服资料。

本政策随产品功能和数据处理方式变化而更新。发布新版本时会更新页面日期；涉及新增处理或权限的变化会按实际需要说明和征得选择。隐私问题请联系 xghbqyxx@163.com。

English

Scope and contact

This policy applies to SuperStar. Developer and operator: 杨星星 / Yang Xingxing. Effective date: 2026-11-05. Privacy contact: xghbqyxx@163.com.

SuperStar is a planning and progress journal. Its core features work on your device without an account. Opening a course website, choosing a cloud-based file or sharing content may use network services provided by your system or the destination you choose.

Information stored on your device

Goals, tasks, schedules, timer and manually entered time, completion states, notes, weekly reflections and images you choose are stored in the app’s local data. Display preferences such as theme and interface language are also saved locally.

Images are processed and compressed on your device. The saved attachment is a compressed copy, not a backup of the original image. You do not need to provide your name, phone number or an account to use these core features.

No automatic upload to the developer

The current version has no developer-operated server for your records. It does not automatically upload your plans, notes, images or activity records, and it contains no third-party advertising, analytics, tracking or AI-processing SDK.

With your permission, reminders are scheduled locally by the operating system, rather than sent by our server. You can manage notification permission in system settings. Local records remain available when reminders are off.

Files, backups, restore and system backup

The app reads a plan, backup or image only after you choose the file. A file provider may be local storage, a system cloud drive or a third-party service you configure. Its access and transfers are governed by your system settings and that provider’s rules.

An exported JSON backup contains plans, records, notes, reflections and compressed image data. The app does not add password encryption to this backup. Treat it as a sensitive file and do not upload it publicly.

The app may keep a local recovery copy before a restore or course-content update to protect existing records. System backups, device migration or a cloud drive you choose may retain copies according to the platform version, your settings and the service involved. The Android build is configured to disable automatic system backup; manual exports and actual device migration behavior remain subject to the operating system. We do not claim that data can never appear in any cloud backup.

Sharing and learning links you choose

When you share a summary or export a file, you choose the recipient and destination. The receiving app, cloud drive or platform processes that content under its own rules. SuperStar does not automatically publish your content to social platforms.

In version 1.1, web links open in your system browser. From version 1.2, ordinary web links in task materials, instructions, outcome descriptions and note previews normally open in the in-app browser only when you tap them. You can also choose your system browser. Web pages require a network connection. The in-app browser uses a temporary session; its cookies and cache are not included in plan backups, and the app does not persist web browsing history. External websites may still process network, account or browsing information under their own privacy policies. SuperStar does not automatically attach your local notes or images to web requests. The host of this policy or support page may also process requests according to its own policies.

Retention, editing and deletion

You can edit local notes and records, remove attachments and manage reminders. Removing an attachment or changing current data does not automatically delete earlier exports, content already shared with others, system or cloud backups, or local recovery copies.

To clear all local app data, use the operating system’s option for deleting the app and its data, and separately manage any exports and backups you retain. An option that removes the app while preserving documents is not the same as erasing data. We do not hold a server copy of your local records and cannot remotely recover or delete them.

Support email and policy changes

If you email support, we process the address, description and attachments you choose to send to reply and investigate the issue. Do not send identity documents, passwords, a complete private backup or unnecessary personal information. You may use the same address to request handling of support information you provided.

We update this policy when features or data handling change and update the date when publishing it. New processing or permissions will be explained and choices requested where applicable. Contact xghbqyxx@163.com with privacy questions.
